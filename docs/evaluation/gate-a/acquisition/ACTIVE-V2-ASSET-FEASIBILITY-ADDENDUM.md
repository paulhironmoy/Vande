## What v2 changes—and what it does not

V2 corrects two static-composition defects:

- the field PIP now has a separate batter plus exactly a keeper, bowler and
  nine other fielders with stable identities; only the bowler is highlighted,
  with bowler-end at the top and striker-end at the bottom;
- HTML and Unity use one centre-bottom `cover` crop contract, anchored at
  `(0.5, 1.0)` in top-left normalized coordinates.

V2 imports no production asset and does not add or accept a licence, rig,
animation, authentic recording, production font, runtime binding or mobile
measurement. The source reference is unchanged. The corrected PIP makes a
future identity test precise; the corrected crop makes a future framing test
precise. Neither creates the production content needed to pass those tests.

## Acquisition and promotion boundary

No purchase, import, commissioning, account/terms acceptance or asset-source
change is authorized by this addendum. A future consolidated proposal must bind
the exact source and archived rights, price when current, editable originals,
expected compressed/installed/runtime cost, exact import delta, contribution to
the accepted frame and a free alternative with its trade-off.

Gate A cannot pass until production-feasible assets and their runtime bindings
exist, exact-build Android and variant measurements pass, an independent
reviewer accepts the complete package, and the owner approves the exact visual
direction. Until then the active target remains a static-direction candidate.
