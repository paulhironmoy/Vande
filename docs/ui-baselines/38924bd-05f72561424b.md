VANDE is **2.1 points behind the UI leader** in this comparator set. Its navigation/task flow is already competitive,
but its live-screen UI is only **4.8/10**: the field overlay occupies too much of the play corridor, live information is
weak at glance size, control affordances are not visibly taught in the captured state, and React and Unity read as two
different visual systems.

The 6.2 score must not be interpreted as the quality of VANDE's players, stadium, animation or match atmosphere. Those
are deliberately excluded. This prevents improved 3D art from hiding a weak interface, and prevents the present
procedural art from unfairly lowering an interface-only score.

## Independent check and uncertainty

One independent evaluator applied the fixed rubric above. A second evaluator used a nearby eight-dimension weighting
and returned VANDE 6.6, Real Cricket 24 7.4, WCC3 7.0 and Cricket League 8.8. The ranking and diagnosis were unchanged.
The retained fixed-rubric estimates are:

- VANDE: +/-0.5.
- Cricket League: +/-0.7.
- Real Cricket 24 and WCC3: +/-0.8.

Do not claim a meaningful gain for a movement smaller than the applicable uncertainty. No score of 9 or higher is
defensible without the same scripted flow on matched devices, including text scaling, contrast, safe areas, touch
targets, interruption/recovery and live-play states.
