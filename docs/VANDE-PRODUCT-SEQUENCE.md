# VANDE product sequence: the product constitution

The owner set this sequence on 2026-10-26 (brief: `docs/prompts/VANDE-PRODUCT-SEQUENCE-BRIEF.md`). It overrides
earlier feature pressure, agent enthusiasm and speculative expansion.

**Active stage:** Stage 1 (Practice quality). Stage 0 passed independent review on measured build
`38924bd+bd015d7cdfd9` at 2026-10-26 11:02Z. The evidence and limitations are in
`release/product-sequence/stage-0/independent/STAGE-0-VERDICT-38924bd+bd015d7cdfd9.md`.
This documentation-only transition does not certify a new runtime build or pass the Stage 1 quality gate.

**Stages 2–6 remain CLOSED.** No agent may build, start or advance work for a later stage. Ideas for later stages
go to the not-now register (§9) through the lead.

```
PRACTICE QUALITY → REPEAT DESIRE → CHASE TENSION → ASYNCHRONOUS COMPETITION → DAILY RITUAL → SOCIAL GROWTH
   (Stage 1)         (Stage 2)        (Stage 3)           (Stage 4)              (Stage 5)      (Stage 6)
```

**Non-negotiable rule:** no stage starts until the stage before it passes its exit gate with reproducible evidence.

Never confuse:

- code completed with experience completed;
- automated tests with player validation;
- feature quantity with product quality;
- visual spectacle with cricket authenticity;
- retention mechanics with genuine repeat desire.

A later-stage feature must never hide a weakness in an earlier stage.

**Order of authority, highest first:**

1. This document.
2. `docs/VANDE-EXPERIENCE-CONSTITUTION.md`, the experience laws.
3. `docs/prompts/VANDE-INTERFACE-ASSET-CONSTITUTION.md`, binding for screens and assets.
4. The feature briefs in `docs/prompts/`.

**Scoring law for every gate:** the locked rubrics.

| Rubric | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| `release/player-experience/ceiling-rubric.md` | Rules R1–R11, the criteria catalogue, the six ceiling dimensions |
| `release/player-experience/DUAL-CEILING-RUBRIC.md` | New Player X (rookie) and Expert Player X |
| `release/competitive-experience/COMPETITIVE-RUBRIC.md` | Sixteen dimensions and the competitor comparison |

Labels:

- **VERIFIED:** reproducible evidence, including real players where the gate needs them.
- **PROVISIONAL:** internal or automated evaluation only. Any score above 8.8 without real players is at most
  PROVISIONAL.
- **UNPROVEN:** no adequate evidence.

## 2–5. Gates, allowed and prohibited features, evidence

### Stage 0: restore a trustworthy baseline

- **Entry:** now.
- **Exit gate:** all of these hold on one build id (`release/player-experience/tools/build-id.sh`), recorded in
  `stage-0/`.
  1. `npm run typecheck:all` passes.
  2. The production web build (`npm run build`) passes, within the bundle budget.
  3. The Android debug build installs and launches on the supported emulator configuration, and the lifecycle smoke
     test passes (`scripts/android-practice.sh`).
  4. The unit and integration suites pass (`npm test`, `npm run test:render`).
  5. The Practice e2e passes (`--project practice`). The legacy suites (`chromium`, `social`, `flow`) pass or record
     an owner-accepted change of contract.
     - Stale expectations are removed only when the product contract has genuinely changed.
     - Each such change is recorded in the decision log (§8).
  6. One uninterrupted Practice over completes in the shipped build: a recorded run, with no console errors.
  7. The baseline is recorded:
     - frame time over complete deliveries per tier;
     - startup time (launch to interactive, and launch to first delivery);
     - crash and error count;
     - device coverage.
  8. A system check, not isolated passes. The over in item 6 runs on the integrated tree.
- **Allowed:** fixes, integration, test repair and measurement.
- **Prohibited:** new features of any stage, broad rewrites, and deleting functioning code or tests to get green.

### Stage 1: Practice quality (the current priority)

- **Entry:** Stage 0 has passed.
- **Required launch flow:**

  ```
  Launch
  └── Practice
      ├── Predefined Over
      └── Random Over
  ```

- **Exit gate, automated** (scored by the independent evaluators with the locked rubrics):
  - Typecheck, the relevant tests and the production builds pass, web and Android.
  - No Critical or High Practice defect is open (`release/player-experience/shared/technical/FINDINGS.md`).
  - Input-to-feedback latency and frame pacing meet the budgets in §7 (CTL1, PERF1–PERF3).
  - Deterministic replay passes (FAIR1, FAIR2, SYS2).
  - The rendered delivery agrees with the physics state (COH1–COH7).
  - Coach explanations agree with the simulation data (FAIR3, COACH2, COACH5).
  - No unexplained outcome manipulation (SYS1, FAIR5, CTL8).
  - No dominant universal shot (FAIR6, EX7).
- **Exit gate, human:**
  - At least 10 New Player X and 10 Expert Player X testers, on representative Indian Android phones, not desktop
    alone.
  - Behaviour is observed, not only rated.
  - A pilot of 3 + 3 comes first.
  - The package is in `release/player-experience/shared/playtest/`.
- **Targets:**
  - New Player X ≥ 9.0, with no critical comprehension failure.
  - Expert Player X ≥ 9.0, with no critical authenticity failure.
  - Practice quality ≥ 9.2 after evidence review, computed as the ceiling rubric's Player experience dimension
    (§6.1).
  - The players are never averaged; both must pass independently.
  - The long-term ceilings stay at 9.5 (dual rubric). The Stage 1 gate is 9.0 / 9.0 / 9.2.
- **Allowed:** everything Practice needs.
  - Batter-view perception, and readable release, line, length, pace, bounce, swing, seam and spin.
  - Responsive shot selection and timing. Visible cause from input to bat, contact and result.
  - A real field and credible scoring, with one wicket ending the innings, up to ten overs.
  - Exact-ball retry.
  - Coach Mode: what the ball did, what you did, why, the better response, when to play, defend or leave, and how to
    replay the same ball correctly.
  - Mastery-true progress (form and mastery as reports).
  - Premium artifacts: bat, ball, seam, stumps, pitch, kit, players, stadium, field.
  - Proportional audio.
  - A minimal interface, with setup visible without covering the delivery.
  - Premium, Balanced and Classic tiers.
  - Pitch surfaces.
  - Tester-mode instrumentation for the study.
  - Competitor benchmarking as evaluation only.
- **Prohibited:**
  - Chase (a "Coming next" teaser is also off the launch in Stage 1);
  - Today's Over or daily anything;
  - challenges, links or duels;
  - rivalries, tournaments, leaderboards;
  - share cards, highlights, notifications;
  - accounts, monetization, live multiplayer;
  - content scale beyond what the gate needs;
  - streaks or rewards.

### Stage 2: Repeat desire

- **Entry:** Stage 1 signed off in the decision log.
- **Exit gate:**
  - Observed repeat behaviour from both player types, measured without rewards as the primary reason:
    - voluntary replay;
    - the second-over start rate;
    - session continuation;
    - exact-ball retry use;
    - improvement across attempts;
    - next-day return.
  - Thresholds are frozen in a stage rubric before Stage 2 starts.
- **Allowed:**
  - exact-ball replay polish;
  - instant over restart;
  - personal bests;
  - delivery mastery;
  - meaningful form progression;
  - optional coach goals;
  - progressive, understandable delivery difficulty;
  - a short post-over reflection;
  - the choice between correcting a weakness and facing a fresh over.
- **Prohibited:** streak pressure, prizes, social obligation, and everything that belongs to Stages 3–6.

### Stage 3: Chase tension

- **Entry:** Stage 2 has passed. The launch then gains Chase, with 1-, 2- and 5-over challenges.
- **Exit gate:**
  - Players understand the match state.
  - Tension rises as the chase narrows.
  - Wins feel earned, and losses create a clear desire to retry.
  - Experts cannot detect outcome steering.
  - All of it measured by evaluators and real players.
- **Allowed:**
  - The target and legible runs, balls and wickets.
  - Pressure rising from the match state.
  - Credible field and bowling responses.
  - Fair attribution of a loss.
  - Close finishes from cricket systems only.
  - A fast restart.
  - Proportional boundary, wicket and final-ball presentation.
  - Reuse of the proven batting system only.
- **Prohibited:**
  - a separate arcade engine;
  - scripted finishes;
  - Stage 4–6 features.

### Stage 4: Asynchronous competition

- **Entry:** Stage 3 has passed.
- **Design:** already drafted in `docs/growth/`, as design only. The physics Sealed Over will replace the bowling-plan
  Sealed Six through a feature-flagged migration (owner decision, 2026-10-26).
- **Exit gate:** groups complete challenges reliably, without coordination, confusion or manual intervention.
- **Allowed:**
  - Challenge creation, with a compact identity and payload.
  - Entry by a clean link or code.
  - Versioned, fair conditions.
  - Deadlines, and handling for players who never join.
  - An automatic reveal that does not depend on the initiator.
  - Tie-breakers disclosed before play (completion time may stay concealed until the reveal).
  - Results comparison and rematch.
  - Privacy controls.
  - Replay integrity and anti-tampering checks.
- **Prohibited:**
  - WhatsApp as a database or a notification channel;
  - Stage 5–6 features.

### Stage 5: Daily ritual

- **Entry:** asynchronous play is dependable.
- **Exit gate:**
  - D1, D7 and D30 return, measured separately for New and Expert Player X.
  - Daily completion, the source of the return, and time to the first ball.
- **Allowed:**
  - Today's Over, with the same daily conditions for everyone;
  - a daily delivery theme;
  - a skill-practice recommendation;
  - a light form history;
  - an optional friend comparison;
  - a recovery-friendly streak.
- **Prohibited:**
  - punishment for missing a day;
  - fabricated urgency;
  - countdowns;
  - reward inflation.

### Stage 6: Social growth

- **Entry:** players already return for the game itself.
- **Exit gate:**
  - Shares per completed session, challenge acceptance, recipient activation and rematches.
  - Group mute and complaint signals.
  - Retained users from shares, not raw link clicks.
- **Allowed:**
  - result cards;
  - deterministic replays and highlights;
  - close-finish summaries;
  - personal-best shares;
  - challenge and rematch links;
  - group tournaments;
  - opt-in leaderboards;
  - highlight export for creators and top performers;
  - YouTube publishing only with explicit consent and moderation.
- **Prohibited:**
  - noisy repeated messages;
  - private identity or performance exposed without consent;
  - a recipient experience that needs explaining.

## 6. New Player X and Expert Player X

The definitions and rubrics are in `release/player-experience/DUAL-CEILING-RUBRIC.md` §1, §3 and §4.

- **New Player X** (rookie rubric R1–R7, RK1–RK6) must show, observed:
  - begins without help;
  - completes the first over;
  - explains the basic reason for the major outcomes;
  - perceives meaningful control;
  - is not overwhelmed by setup or the HUD;
  - voluntarily chooses a replay or another over.
- **Expert Player X** (expert rubric E1–E7, EX1–EX11) must show:
  - distinguishes delivery types;
  - recognizes credible cricket causality;
  - changes shot strategy with the ball;
  - finds exact-ball replay useful;
  - perceives depth beyond timing;
  - voluntarily tries to improve.
- **Both:** the same physics, with no hidden help (SYS1, SYS2). The expert can skip all teaching in one tap (X1), and
  the rookie sees depth only when it becomes relevant (X2).

## 7. Performance, accessibility and supported devices

- **Tiers:**
  - Premium, Balanced and Classic, with the same physics, scoring and ball readability on every tier (READ3).
  - Quality changes only between deliveries (PERF2).
  - No technical wording on player-facing screens (F8).
  - An unsupported device never enters a broken 3D session, and Classic is a complete game.
- **Budgets, measured over complete deliveries** (ceiling rubric PERF1 and CTL1):
  - Premium: p95 ≤ 18 ms.
  - Balanced: p95 ≤ 22.2 ms and p99 ≤ 33.3 ms.
  - Classic: p95 ≤ 33.3 ms.
  - No frame over 100 ms inside a delivery.
  - Input to visible bat movement: p95 ≤ 50 ms on Premium and Balanced, ≤ 67 ms on Classic.
  - Identical results at 30 and 60 fps (PERF3).
  - Nothing loads during a delivery (PERF4).
- **Timing forgiveness (binding):**
  - Learn and Play keep the declared bands plus a declared device allowance.
  - Real has none on any device.
  - Forgiveness is latched per over, recorded per delivery, and shown when it changes.
- **Accessibility:**
  - 200% text;
  - keyboard operation, including attack;
  - screen-reader semantics;
  - reduced motion;
  - captions;
  - play with audio muted or haptics off;
  - colour-safe status;
  - 48 dp touch targets.
- **Supported devices:**
  - Android minSdk 28, in landscape at the five viewports in ceiling rubric §3.
  - Representative Indian Android phones: one budget and one mid-range. Physical phones are not yet available, so the
    D gates are pending.
  - Desktop Chromium.
- **Android distribution gates:**
  - A counted Android configuration must remain at or below **50,000,000 bytes fresh-installed**, measured after
    launch reaches the first playable ball and including delivered splits, native libraries, mandatory assets and
    fresh app data/cache.
  - The same production build must be installable and complete Stage 1 on at least **90.0% of an India-weighted active
    Android-phone denominator**. Unknown, excluded and untested cohorts remain in the denominator and count as zero.
  - Premium, Balanced or the complete Classic fallback may satisfy working reach. Unity must never make the retained
    fallback unavailable on a phone that could otherwise run it.
  - APK/AAB size, model counts and emulator runs are preflight evidence only. The binding calculation and evidence
    contract are in `docs/ANDROID_SIZE_AND_REACH.md`; both gates remain UNPROVEN until that evidence exists.
  - The Android emulator is engineering evidence only.
