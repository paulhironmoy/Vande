The short version: VANDE Cricket needs only a nickname. We never ask for your phone number, email, real name, contacts or location. We keep what the game needs to work fairly, for as long as it needs it. You can export or delete your data at any time from Settings.

Who can play

VANDE Cricket is for players aged 18 and over. The app asks for your year of birth once, on first launch, and keeps only the fact that you confirmed you are an adult, never the year.

What we never collect

No phone number, email address or real name. No contacts or address book. No GPS or precise location. No photos or files. No advertising identifier. There are no ads. Free-text chat is off.

Times other players see are kept coarse on purpose. A finished over or innings shows up for your opponent only after a random delay of a few minutes, so nobody learns exactly when you played.

Optional: anonymous usage statistics

Only if you say yes (you can change your mind in Settings), the app sends a small, fixed set of events, such as "a tutorial was completed", to our analytics provider, with an ID that changes every month and is never linked to your account. Saying no changes nothing in the game.

Official YouTube highlights: only with your consent

If one of your matches makes the Daily Top 100, you can choose to let us publish a short highlight video on the official VANDE Cricket YouTube channel. Nothing is published unless every player in the match says yes and confirms they are 18 or older. The video shows only nicknames and generated avatars. You can withdraw at any time, and the video is removed. Your Top 100 badge is yours whether or not a video is made.

Who processes your data

  • Cloudflare runs our servers and storage (data may be processed outside India on Cloudflare's network).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications, if you turn them on.
  • Google sign-in, only if you link Google to recover your account.
  • PostHog receives usage statistics, only if you opted in.
  • Google YouTube and Google Cloud Run handle highlight videos, only after every player consented.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

  • See and correct your profile in the app (Profile and Settings).
  • Export a copy of your data from Settings → Export my data.
  • Delete your account and data from Settings → Delete my account. It takes effect at once. If you no longer have your phone, see how to delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics, notifications or a highlight at any time.
  • Grievances and questions: write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 30 days.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Sealed bowling plans are encrypted until the reveal. Session secrets are stored only as one-way hashes on our side and in secure storage on your phone. Logs record only which kind of request happened, never who sent it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before the change applies.
