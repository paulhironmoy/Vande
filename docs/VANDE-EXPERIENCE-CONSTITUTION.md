# VANDE experience constitution

The owner set these laws on 2026-10-26 (brief: `docs/prompts/VANDE-GAME-DIRECTOR-BRIEF.md`).

- **Authority:** they rank directly below the product sequence (`docs/VANDE-PRODUCT-SEQUENCE.md`). They govern every
  experience decision in every stage.
- **Screens and assets:** these must also satisfy `docs/prompts/VANDE-INTERFACE-ASSET-CONSTITUTION.md`.
- **Enforcement:**
  - Every law below names the locked rubric criteria that test it (`release/player-experience/ceiling-rubric.md`,
    `DUAL-CEILING-RUBRIC.md`).
  - A violation is a finding, with severity per ceiling rubric §4. Independent evaluators find it, the owner fixes
    it, and the lead arbitrates.

**Core promise:** "I saw the delivery, read it, chose my response, executed it, understood the outcome, improved and
immediately wanted another ball." Every system must strengthen it.

## The ten laws

| # | Law | What it means in VANDE | Tested by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **One obvious first action** | A new player knows at once to watch the delivery, then tap, flick or leave. One short demonstration before the first ball can be skipped and never covers a live ball. | F4, RK1, RK3, F1, F2 |
| 2 | **Simple surface, deep system** | The opening shows only what the next delivery needs. Line, length, pace, swing, seam, spin, footwork, timing, elevation, power, field and risk emerge progressively, and an expert can skip ahead in one tap. | RK2, X1, X2, M1, M2 |
| 3 | **Cricket causality** | Delivery, input, bat motion, contact, exit, fielding, score, sound, haptic, crowd and coaching all describe the same physical event. | COH1–COH11, FAIR3, FAIR4, SYS2 |
| 4 | **No secret outcome manipulation** | No guaranteed boundaries, no altered fixed delivery, no difficulty change during a ball. Assistance is visible, fixed before release, removable (Real has none) and recorded per delivery. | SYS1, FAIR5, FAIR7, EX8, CTL8 |
| 5 | **Every ball is a story** | Anticipation (run-up), recognition (release, flight, bounce), decision, action, consequence, emotional response, understanding (reason and next action), next desire (the next ball one tap away). | READ1–READ4, FBK1–FBK5, F6, RK4 |
| 6 | **Every over has an arc** | Six deliveries build pressure, memory and consequence. The last ball matters because of the five before it, never because of a script. | COH9 (context-driven emotion), FAIR1, FAIR5 |
| 7 | **Failure must teach** | Every failure is caused, explainable and correctable. The identical ball can be retried at once and the improvement shown. | F6, RK4, RK5, COACH1–COACH7 |
| 8 | **Visual realism serves play** | Realistic players, bat, gloves, ball, seam, shine, pitch, stumps, field and crowd add embodiment without ever reducing ball readability. | READ1–READ3, EMB1–EMB5, ART1–ART8 |
| 9 | **Depth never requires clutter** | Expert controls and diagnostics appear only when asked for or earned. Essential match information stays visible and restrained. | M2, M5, U3, F8, X2 |
| 10 | **Respect the player** | No dark patterns, false urgency, deceptive rewards, excessive notifications or monetized frustration. | Prohibited list below |

The product sequence's permanent principles are part of this constitution:

1. one obvious next action;
2. a simple surface over a deep cricket system;
3. every outcome has an understandable cause;
4. Practice improves real in-game decisions;
5. realism serves perception and control;
6. feedback intensity matches the moment;
7. failure teaches and invites another attempt;
8. the field, ball and match state are gameplay information;
9. weak mechanics are never hidden under animation or rewards;
10. respect for the player's time, attention, privacy and intelligence.

## Proportional feedback

| Event | Feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Leave | Restrained |
| Dot | Mild pressure |
| Single | Acknowledgement |
| Four | Strong release |
| Six | Peak celebration, proportional in Practice and never World Cup-final noise for a routine boundary |
| Edge | Tension |
| Near miss | Breath and recovery |
| Wicket | Decisive silence, impact, then a shift in the crowd |
| Exact-ball improvement | A satisfying mastery confirmation |

- The crowd never knows an outcome before the ball physically reaches it: no look-ahead.
- Audio, haptics and animation never contradict the result. Contact sound and haptic come within 20 ms of the
  physical contact, and never on a miss (COH6, COH9, FBK3).

## Performance laws

Premium, Balanced and Classic all have:

- the same physics and scoring;
- the same essential ball readability (READ3).

They are also bound by these laws:

- Quality changes only between deliveries.
- No technical wording appears on player-facing screens.
- No unsupported device enters a broken 3D session.
- Classic is a complete cricket experience, not a punishment.
- Frame times are measured over complete deliveries, never menus (PERF1–PERF5).

## Retention test (from Stage 2 onwards)

Every retention feature must answer yes to this question: would the player return because playing is satisfying,
even if the reward were removed? If not, redesign it.

## Principles borrowed from other games (abstract only)

VANDE copies no code, interfaces, characters, artwork, levels, terminology, branding, sounds or trade dress. Each
principle is implemented in VANDE's own cricket terms, and only in the stage that allows it.

| Principle | VANDE form | Stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immediate clarity, rich consequence, rapid retry (Candy Crush) | Tap, flick or leave; crisp contact; exact-ball retry; progressive depth | 1 |
| One tactile gesture with physical meaning (Monster Strike) | Gesture direction, distance, timing and release visibly control the bat, consistently | 1 |
| Responsiveness and calibration (COD Mobile) | No perceptible UI hesitation; touch, mouse, trackpad and keyboard calibrated independently; synchronized contact feedback | 1 |
| Uncertainty and player stories (PUBG) | Unknown deliveries hidden until release; real risk; an over as a memorable sequence | 1 |
| Short readable skill sessions (Brawl Stars) | One complete, satisfying over; expert control without complicating the rookie surface | 1 |
| Interaction polish (Royal Match) | No dead taps, delayed responses, layout shifts or confusing exits | 1 |
| Atmosphere and artifacts (Genshin Impact) | Premium stadium, pitch, equipment and players; audio presence | 1 |
| Mastery ceiling (Honor of Kings) | No dominant universal input; experts measurably beat beginner and random play | 1 |
| Simple input, strategic depth (Puzzle & Dragons) | One gesture surface over a deep shot and field model | 1 |
| Character attachment (Fate/Grand Order) | Original bowlers with styles and histories | 2 (not now) |
| Ownership and rivalry (Clash of Clans) | Form profile, rival histories, groups | 2 and 4–6 (not now) |
| A daily ritual (Pokémon GO) | Today's Over, with no penalty for missing a day | 5 (not now) |
| Celebration and social reaction (Monopoly GO) | Over summaries, rematches, shareable highlights, proportional celebration | 2 and 6 (not now) |
| Friend participation (Ludo King) | Simple WhatsApp invitations, clear reveal and expiry rules, no group spam | 4 and 6 (not now) |
| Near-zero onboarding (Subway Surfers) | Launch straight into batting; teach through play | 1 |

## Prohibited in every stage

- lives or energy;
- multiple or excessive currencies;
- false scarcity or fake countdowns;
- pay-to-win;
- intrusive or mandatory advertising;
- notification pressure, or asking for permissions before the player has had value;
- punitive streaks;
- deceptive rewards;
- monetized frustration;
- fake opponents or fake social activity;
- hidden outcome steering;
- unlicensed or placeholder player-facing assets.
