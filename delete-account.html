Delete your VANDE Cricket account

VANDE Cricket · operated by {{OPERATOR}}

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open VANDE Cricket on your phone or at this website.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion takes effect at once:

  • your profile, sign-in links, notification tokens, groups, friends, scores and streaks are deleted;
  • in past matches, cups and rooms you become "Deleted player";
  • any highlight you consented to is withdrawn and removed from YouTube.

A scrubbed placeholder of the account row is purged after 30 days.

If you no longer have your phone

  • You linked Google or a passkey: open this website, choose "Already played? Recover your account", sign in, then use Settings → Delete my account.
  • You played as a guest only: your account holds no contact details, so we cannot check that it is yours. Write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your nickname and, if you can, the code of a match or invite link you shared. We will remove what we can safely match to you within 30 days. Guest data is otherwise kept only as long as listed in our privacy policy.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Match replays keep the over you bowled and the shots you played under "Deleted player", so your opponent's result stays verifiable. Security records are kept for their stated period (rate-limit keys 2 days, a pseudonymous audit log 180 days).
